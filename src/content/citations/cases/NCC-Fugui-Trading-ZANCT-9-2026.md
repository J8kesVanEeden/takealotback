# National Consumer Commission v Fugui Trading (Pty) Ltd
## [2026] ZANCT 9 | National Consumer Tribunal | 25 May 2026

The NCC referred a retailer to the Tribunal over notices displayed in its shop. The matter was unopposed.

## Proposition cited on the site

A displayed return policy of exchange only, not money back, is itself an unfair term under CPA s 48(1)(a)(ii); no individual transaction has to be proved. The Tribunal found prohibited conduct on that and a separate breach (s 24(1) read with regulation 6(1)(a), trade descriptions) and imposed a R100,000 administrative fine:

> [45] "The respondent contravened section 24(1) read with regulation 6(1)(a) and section 48(1)(a)(ii). On this basis, the respondent is found to have engaged in prohibited conduct. The Tribunal finds that an administrative fine is an appropriate punitive measure …"

## Verbatim extracts (SAFLII, opened 2026-11-10)

> [34] "… The evidence before the Tribunal indicate a sign displayed on the respondent's premises stating 'NO MONEY BACK' and 'ATTENTION ALL CUSTOMERS - You are allowed to return a item for another item not for money - Thank you'."

> [36] "Section 48(1)(a)(ii) states that a supplier must not offer to supply, supply or enter into an agreement to supply any goods or services on terms that are unfair, unreasonable or unjust. In this case, no particular transaction is required to prove the respondent's contravention, as the applicant has persuaded the Tribunal that by displaying the notices on its premises, the respondent was offering to supply its goods to consumers on terms that are unfair, unreasonable and unjust. In doing so, the respondent has contravened section 48(1)(a)(ii)."

## Scope and limits

- Unopposed; a physical shop, not an online retailer.
- The Commission's separate ss 20/51/56 allegations failed because no specific transaction was proved (para 35). The site cites the case **for s 48 only**.

## Site reliance

- Clause 06 (credit vs refund): a policy that defaults to credit where the law gives a refund rests on the same logic as an exchange-only notice.
