# Magistrates' Courts monetary jurisdiction (2014 determinations)
## GoN 216 and GoN 217 · Government Gazette 37477 · 27 March 2014 · in force 1 June 2014

The Minister of Justice and Constitutional Development set the money limits for civil claims in the Magistrates' Courts under s 29(1) of the Magistrates' Courts Act 32 of 1944. GoN 217 covers the district courts; GoN 216 covers the regional divisions. Both took effect on 1 June 2014.

**Figures the site states:** District Magistrates' Court up to **R200,000**; Regional Magistrates' Court **above R200,000 up to R400,000**; above R400,000, the High Court.

**Read on 2026-11-18** from the Department of Justice's own PDFs of the gazette (curl + pdftotext). The figures were also checked on 2026-11-10 (`_handoff/refresh-2026-11/B-source-recheck.md`, R7).

**Newer notice?** On 2026-11-17 the Department's notice list (`justice.gov.za/legislation/notices/notice_list.html`, entries to 26 October 2026) showed Magistrates' Courts Act notices only on areas, places of sitting and rules, none on monetary jurisdiction, and a web search found no later determination (`_handoff/refresh-2026-11-17/raw/doj-thresholds-check-2026-11-17.txt`). The Department's list is its own index, not the full Gazette.

## GoN 217 — courts for districts (verbatim)

> "I Jeffrey Thamsaqa Radebe, Minister of Justice and Constitutional Development, acting under section 29 (1 (a), (b), (d), (e), (f) and (g), 46 (2) (c) (i), (ii) and (iii), 50 (1) 65 1 (3), 74 (1) (b) and (2) and 92 (1) (b) of the Magistrates' Court Act, 1944 (Act No. 32 of 1944) hereby, with effect from 1 June 2014, determine for the purposes of the section of the said Act mentioned in column "A" of the Schedule, the amount mentioned opposite thereto in Column "B" of the Schedule."

Schedule (extract):

| Column A | Column B |
|---|---|
| Section 29 (1) (a) actions in which is claimed the delivery or transfer of any property, movable or immovable | R200 000 |
| Section 29 (1)(g) actions other than those already mentioned in this section | R200 000 |

(The scanned gazette's text layer reads the (g) row as "8200 000"; the printed figure is R200 000, as in every other row.)

## GoN 216 — regional divisions (verbatim)

> "I Jeffrey Thamsacia Radebe, Minister of Justice and Constitutional Development, acting under section 29 (1 (a), (b), (d), (e), (f) and (g) of the Magistrates' Court Act, 1944 (Act No. 32 of 1944) hereby with effect from 1 June 2014 - … (b) determine, for the purposes of the section of the said Act mentioned in column "A" of the Schedule, the amount mentioned opposite thereto in Column "B" of the Schedule."

Schedule (extract):

| Column A | Column B |
|---|---|
| Section 29 (1)(a) actions for the delivery or transfer of any property, movable or immovable | Above R200 000 up to R400 000 |
| Section 29 (1)(g) actions other than those already mentioned in section 29(1) | Above R200 000 up to R400 000 |

(The text layer reads the (g) row as "up to 8400 000"; every other row reads "R400 000".) The minister's name is spelt differently in the two notices' text layers ("Thamsaqa", "Thamsacia"); quoted as extracted.
