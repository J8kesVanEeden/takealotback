# NCC media statement — Black Friday 2024
## 28 November 2024 · National Consumer Commission

The NCC's newest Black Friday statement found on thencc.org.za as of 17 November 2026. No 2025 or 2026 Black Friday statement was found (searched 2026-11-17). If the NCC issues one before 27 November 2026, it replaces this one on the site.

## Why the site cites this

It is the regulator's own reminder, issued for Black Friday, that a supplier must not mislead about a price's relationship to an earlier price, and its advice to shoppers to know an item's original price. The site uses it in Clause 23 (sale pricing and sold-out deals) and the Black Friday FAQ, next to CPA s 29(b)(iii) and s 41(3)(f).

## Verbatim extracts (PDF text, read 2026-11-17)

> "The National Consumer Commission (NCC) warns consumers to exercise caution when interacting with certain suppliers during this Black Friday, the festive season and beyond."

> "Suppliers are reminded of their obligation in terms of the CPA. This includes not marketing their offerings in a misleading or deceptive manner. The CPA requires that “when a retailer or service provider markets the goods, they must do so in a manner that is not misleading or deceiving including the condition of the goods, the price at which the goods may be supplied, or the relationship of the price to any previous price or competitor’s price for comparable or similar goods.”"

> "On the upcoming Black Friday, consumers are further reminded that knowing the original prices of items they intend buying during this period will help to determine if an item is indeed discounted or not. Consumers are urged to be very vigilant when transacting online as some of the online stores are masquerading as legitimate businesses while they are effectively out to scam consumers."

The rest of the statement concerns about 40 suppliers the NCC could not trace after they took payment and did not deliver. The site does not rely on that part.

## Note

The NCC's quotation marks around its summary of the CPA are its own. The Act's actual wording is CPA s 29(b)(iii), quoted verbatim in [CPA-2008.md](../statutes/CPA-2008.md).
