# CPA juristic-person threshold: R2 million
## Government Notice 294 · Government Gazette 34181 · 1 April 2011

CPA s 5(2)(b) says the Act does not apply to a transaction in which "the consumer is a juristic person whose asset value or annual turnover, at the time of the transaction, equals or exceeds the threshold value determined by the Minister in terms of section 6" (verbatim in [CPA-2008.md](../statutes/CPA-2008.md)). This notice is that determination.

**Read on 2026-11-18** from the gazette PDF on gov.za (PDF title "Consumer Protection Act: Determination of threshold applicable to size of juristic person"; curl + pdftotext).

**Still current?** No later s 6 determination was found on 2026-11-18. A web search turned up none, and 2026 commentary still gives R2 million and says the figure has not been revised since 2011. The Government Gazette index itself was not searched. CPA s 2(5) keeps a threshold in effect until a later one takes effect.

## Verbatim

> "In terms of section 6 (1) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2008 (Act No. 68 of 2008) (''the Act"), the Minister of the Department of Trade and Industry, by notice in the Gazette, must determine a monetary threshold applicable to the size of the juristic person for the purposes of the section 5(2) (b). In terms of section 5(2)(b) thereof, the Act does not apply to any transaction in terms of which the consumer is a juristic person whose asset value or annual turnover, at the time of the transaction equals or exceeds the threshold value determined by the Minister in terms of section 6."

> "I, Dr Rob Davies, Minister of Trade and Industry, do hereby determine in terms of section 6 (1) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2008 (Act 68 of 2008) that the monetary threshold applicable to the size of the juristic person in terms of section 5 (2) (b) is R2 000 000.00 (Two Million Rands). The afore-mentioned monetary threshold must be calculated in accordance with the attached schedule."

The schedule ("Threshold determination: method of calculation") says assets and turnover are calculated under South African generally accepted accounting standards: asset value is gross assets with no deduction for liabilities (item 2), and annual turnover is gross revenue from the sale of goods, the rendering of services and the use by others of the juristic person's assets (item 3), taken from audited financial statements where the juristic person has or must have them (item 4).

## Site reliance

- `/limits`: a buyer that is itself a juristic person (a company, close corporation or trust, for example) with asset value or annual turnover at or above R2 million is outside the CPA. Individual consumers are not affected by the threshold.
- CGSO Advisory Note 11 (para 5.1(ii)) mentions the figure as "an entity with a yearly turnover exceeding R2 million"; the notice's test is asset value **or** annual turnover that **equals or exceeds** R2 million.
