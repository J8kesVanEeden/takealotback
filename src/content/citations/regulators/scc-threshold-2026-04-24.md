# Small Claims Court — monetary jurisdiction (verified 2026-11-10)

## Current threshold

**Up to R30,000, with effect from 1 August 2026.**

Primary source: Government Gazette No. 55038, Government Notice 7717, 20 July 2026 (scanned PDF on justice.gov.za, read 2026-11-10):

> "NOTICE UNDER SECTIONS 15 AND 16 OF THE SMALL CLAIMS COURTS ACT, 1984 (ACT NO. 61 OF 1984): DETERMINATION OF AMOUNT
> 1. I, Ms M T Kubayi, Minister of Justice and Constitutional Development, acting under sections 15 and 16 of the Small Claims Courts Act, 1984 (Act No 61 of 1984), hereby determine R30 000-00, to be the amount for purposes of the said sections with effect from 1 August 2026.
> 2. Government Notice No. R. 296 of 5 March 2019 is hereby withdrawn with effect from 1 August 2026."

Signed by the Minister, dated 15/07/2026.

Corroboration, Department of Justice Small Claims page (`justice.gov.za/scc/scc.htm`, fetched 2026-11-10):

> "An amount not exceeding R 30 000. (This amount is determined by the Minister in the Government Gazette, GG 55038, GoN 7717, 20 July 2026), which comes into effect on 01 Aug 2026."

If your claim is above R30,000, the DoJ page says you can "institute a claim for a lesser amount to pursue your case in the Small Claims Court" (i.e. abandon the excess).

## Representation rule

**No legal representation permitted.** Consumers (and suppliers) represent themselves. Commissioners (typically admitted attorneys or advocates) sit in their personal capacity without remuneration.

## Filing costs

**No court fee to issue a summons.** The Small Claims Court Rules (GN R2573 of 2022) list only one court fee — a photocopy charge — in Annexure 2 Part I. As amended by GoN R6755 (GG 53572, 24 October 2025; in force 28 November 2025):

> "PART I — Court fees — For a copy of a record made by the clerk of the court— for every photocopy of an A4 size page or part thereof: [R2,00] R3,00."

**You can serve the summons yourself.** The DoJ Small Claims page: "The summons can be delivered by you or the sheriff of the court."

**If the sheriff serves it, the fee is set by tariff** (same notice, Part II):

> "1. (a) For the service of a summons, including the registration of the summons on receipt thereof for service: [R75,00] R79,00."

> "3. (a) The sheriff shall, in addition to the fees mentioned in items 1(a), 1(b), 2(a) and 2(b) … be allowed a travelling allowance of R7,50 per kilometre, or part thereof, for the shortest possible forward and return journey from the office of the sheriff to the place of service or execution and back."

> "4. These Rules come into operation on 28 November 2025."

Source: https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/notices/2025/20251024-gg53572rg11899gon6755-RB-SCC.pdf (read 2026-11-10). Until 2026-11-10 one part of the site (the Bodies card) said "~R100 filing fee"; no such fee exists in the Act or the Rules, and it was corrected.

## Languages

All eleven official languages may be used in a Small Claims Court.
