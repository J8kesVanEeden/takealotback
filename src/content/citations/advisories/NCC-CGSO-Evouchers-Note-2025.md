# NCC + CGSO explanatory note: e-commerce platforms and e-vouchers
## 13 July 2025 · National Consumer Commission and Consumer Goods and Services Ombud

A 4-page note issued jointly by the NCC and the CGSO, dated 13 July 2025 (PDF creation date 8 September 2025). It is listed for download on the CGSO's Advisory Notes page, under the banner "Advisory Notes are under review, they will be reuploaded once the review is completed." An earlier URL (`/cgso/download/transacting-with-ecommerce-platforms-and-e-vouchers/`) returned 404 on 2026-11-10; the URL above worked on 2026-11-17.

**Status:** guidance, not law. It predates the two regulators' March 2026 joint paper ([NCC-CGSO-Ecommerce-Paper-2026.md](./NCC-CGSO-Ecommerce-Paper-2026.md)), which states the platform-responsibility view again.

## Why the site cites this

1. **Clause 15 (marketplace sellers):** a second, earlier regulator statement that the hosting platform must resolve a dispute about a third-party seller's item.
2. **Clause 11 (coupons and vouchers):** where one item in a voucher-paid order is unavailable, the regulators say the voucher's full value must go to the rest of that order; the supplier may not hold it back for a future date. Takealot's returns policy instead loads the voucher value "as non-refundable credit" when you "return or cancel your whole order or some of the items in your order" ([takealot-returns-2026-04-24.md](../policies/takealot-returns-2026-04-24.md)).

## Verbatim extracts (PDF text, read 2026-11-17)

### Platform responsibility

> "The Consumer Goods and Services Ombud (CGSO) and the National Consumer Commission (NCC) are of the view that it remains the responsibility of the hosting platform to resolve a dispute that may arise and to ensure that these third-party suppliers comply with all provisions of the CPA and ECTA."

### Vouchers ("Your Voucher Rights Under the CPA")

> "• Validity: Vouchers must be valid for at least 3 years from the date of issue, unless fully redeemed earlier.
> • Ownership: The value of a voucher remains the property of the holder until redeemed.
> • Usage: Suppliers must honour the voucher until full value is used, or expiry occurs after 3 years.
> • Allocation: The value of a voucher is to be redeemed against the goods or services chosen by the consumer at the time of purchase.
> • Unavailability of Goods or Services: Should an item be unavailable, the full value of the voucher must be applied to the remainder of the goods that form part of the collective purchase at the time. The Supplier does not have the discretion to suspend the use of the voucher for a future date."

### Disclaimer

> "This note is provided for informational purposes only and is not legal advice."

The note also quotes CPA s 63 in full (see [CPA-2008.md](../statutes/CPA-2008.md)) and summarises CPA ss 41, 48, 55, 56 and ECT ss 43, 44, 46.
