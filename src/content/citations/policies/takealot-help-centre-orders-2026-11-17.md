# Takealot Help Centre — order cancellation, refunds and missing items
## Snapshot 2026-11-17

Takealot edits these articles often: six customer articles were updated between 6 and 17 November 2026. Each quote below carries the article's own `updated_at` time from Takealot's Help Centre. The site quotes Takealot's own words; where they differ from its terms and conditions, or from the law, the site says so.

Source of the text: Takealot's public Help Centre articles API (`help.takealot.com/api/v2/help_center/en-us/articles.json`), fetched 2026-11-17, HTML stripped. Raw capture kept on file.

## 1. "Why was my order cancelled?" (updated 2026-11-06 13:15 UTC)

URL: https://help.takealot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019252854-Why-was-my-order-cancelled

> "An order may be cancelled if there was a delay in payment, or if we are unable to fulfil the order."

> "Should your order be cancelled after you have made payment, it would usually be due to an issue with stock availability from our supplier. If this was the case, we will communicate via email and credit your account with the full amount you paid (PS: these exclude COD orders)."

**Compare** Takealot's T&Cs (see [takealot-tcs-2026-04-24.md](./takealot-tcs-2026-04-24.md)): "If an order or item is cancelled, we will refund you the amount paid for that cancelled order or item."

## 2. "How do I cancel my order?" (updated 2026-11-15 09:30 UTC)

URL: https://help.takealot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019253774-How-do-I-cancel-my-order

> "If your item is in stock, it is already in the process of being fulfilled. Please allow up to 4 working days for your cancellation request to be processed and your credit or refund applied."

> "Note:
> Delivery fees and TakealotMORE benefits will not be reimbursed as the order is already in the process of being fulfilled."

> "If your order is already with the delivery driver or is being delivered by a courier other than our Takealot Delivery Team, here's what you can do next:
> - accept the delivery,
> - keep the packaging unopened, then
> - log a return for your items."

## 3. "Why is my refund taking so long?" (updated 2026-11-10 04:51 UTC)

URL: https://help.takealot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019253594-Why-is-my-refund-taking-so-long

> "Buy Now, Pay Later Methods (PayJustNow, Payflex): Up to 24 hours
> Loyalty Points (eBucks, Discovery Miles): Up to 24 hours
> Takealot Account Credit & Vouchers: Immediate when processed"

> "Remember: We can only refund the amount you actually paid (excluding coupons/vouchers), and refunds go back to your original payment method whenever possible."

## 4. "What happens if a product is missing from my shipment/delivery?" (updated 2026-11-17 10:35 UTC)

URL: https://help.takealot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019415653-What-happens-if-a-product-is-missing-from-my-shipment-delivery

> "Please contact us within 30 days of delivery/collection."

> "Your Choice:
> We'll ask how you'd prefer to be resolved while we investigate:
> Account Credit
> Refund
> Replacement Item (provided that we have stock available)"

> "If you did not take receipt of the item, please confirm who received order e.g. reception"

> "We will confirm the action, within 24 hours of receiving your request, with one of the following outcomes below:
> Locate the missing items and will schedule delivery to you as soon as possible
> Replace the missing items, by creating a new order
> Credit your takealot.com account with the amount for missing items, if we no longer have stock available"

> "Tell us your preference - credit, refund, or replacement"

**Note:** the article offers a refund as one of three choices, but its list of outcomes names only account credit where there is no stock. The site's Clause 06 and template T20 hold Takealot to the first: you can choose the refund.

## Site reliance

- **Clause 23** and the **Black Friday FAQ**: on a paid order cancelled for stock, Takealot's default is account credit (article 1), although its T&Cs say it will refund.
- **Clause 06** and **template T20**: the missing-item article's own offer of a refund.
- **Delivery-fee FAQ**: article 2's "Delivery fees … will not be reimbursed", and its accept-and-return route for orders already out for delivery.
- **Buy-now-pay-later FAQ**: article 3's refund times for buy-now-pay-later and loyalty points.
