# Takealot privacy policy and PAIA manual: how a customer asks for their own records
## Snapshot 2026-11-17

## Why the site cites this

Template T22 asks Takealot for the records it holds about a customer's own order (proof of delivery, call and chat records) under POPIA s 23. The PAIA manual sends customers to the privacy policy. That policy lists the right and points complaints and questions to the Data & Privacy section of the Help Centre, the route T22 uses. The manual also names the Data Compliance Team for help. The legal right itself is in [POPIA-2013.md](../statutes/POPIA-2013.md) (s 23).

## Privacy policy: "What are your rights under POPI?" (verbatim)

Source: https://terms-and-policies.takealot.com/privacy-policy/, fetched 2026-11-17 (static HTML, tags stripped). Raw capture kept on file.

> "You can:
> request a record or description of your personal information. We might have reasons to say no to some information but you can ask for reasons why;
> ask and receive information about the third-parties who have had access to your personal information;
> ask that we correct inaccurate information. …"

> "we might charge an administrative fee for complex requests."

> "If you are unhappy about how your personal information is handled or have any questions about how we treat your information, contact us through the Data & Privacy section of our Help Centre. We’re happy to help. If we are unable to resolve your complaint, you can complain to the Information Regulator."

## PAIA manual (labelled 2024): "Requesting access to information" (verbatim)

Source: https://static.takealot.com/files/pdfs/PAIA2024.pdf, fetched 2026-11-17 (curl + pdftotext). Raw capture kept on file.

> "Any Requester is advised to contact the Data Compliance Team (DS.Privacy@takealot.com) should the Requester require any assistance in respect of the utilisation of this manual and/or the requesting of documents/information including personal information from Takealot, Superbalist or Mr D Food."

> "A customer of either Takealot, Superbalist or Mr D Food can request access to information through, and in accordance with, the respective Customer Privacy Policies available on the respective webpages."

The manual lists the Information Officer's email as legal@takealot.com, and reserves the prescribed PAIA Form 02 for "All other third parties (general public requests)". A customer therefore uses the privacy-policy route, not Form 02.

## Site reliance

- **Template T22**: send the request through the Data & Privacy section of the Help Centre or to DS.Privacy@takealot.com, copied to legal@takealot.com; ask for a written fee estimate first if any fee is mentioned (POPIA s 23(3)).
