# Competition Commission — Online Intermediation Platforms Market Inquiry (OIPMI)
## Summary of Final Report Findings and Remedial Actions | July 2023

A market inquiry under the Competition Act into online platforms (eCommerce, travel, food delivery, app stores, classifieds and Google Search). The summary is filed here, under commentary, because it is a regulator's report rather than a court ruling (the regulators/ folder is the natural home and it can move there later). Its remedies are competition remedies, not consumer law.

## Proposition cited on the site

Takealot both runs the marketplace and competes on it through Takealot Retail. The Inquiry found that this "creates a conflict of interest" and required Takealot to segregate its Retail division from its Marketplace operations. The site uses this only to undercut a "the seller is solely responsible" deflection; it does not claim the finding gives a consumer any remedy.

## Verbatim extracts (eCommerce section, p 4-5)

> "Whilst Takealot opens its online marketplace to third party sellers, it also trades extensively itself through the Takealot Retail division. This creates a conflict of interest in the same manner as Google, namely it sets the rules for the marketplace and at the same time competes with the marketplace sellers."

> "To address these distortions arising from the conflict of interest, Takealot is required to segregate its Retail division from its Marketplace operations and to prevent its retail services from accessing seller data and unilaterally stopping sellers from competing for certain brands."

> "In addition, Takealot must introduce a 60 day dispute resolution process for marketplace sellers complaints on returns and stock loss which will be deemed resolved in favour of the seller if not resolved within 60 days."

**Correction (2026-11-10).** Earlier site copy dated the finding to August 2023 and said the Inquiry was "recommending the marketplace be operated as a separate business". The summary is dated July 2023, and its wording is that Takealot "is required to segregate" Retail from Marketplace.

## Status

The 60-day seller dispute process and other remedies are seller-facing. Whether and how Takealot implemented the segregation has not been checked for this file.
