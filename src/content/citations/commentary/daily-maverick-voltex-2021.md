# Daily Maverick — CGSO wins membership fee battle

Short summary: explainer on *CGSO NPC v Voltex* [2021] ZAGPPHC 309, confirming the compulsory-participation nature of the CGSO Code under CPA s 82. Useful plain-English corroboration for the site's "CGSO is compulsory" framing.

Re-checked against the live article 2026-11-17 (Georgina Crouth, 7 Apr 2021). Two lines carry the point, verbatim:

> "a high court judgment means all fast-moving consumer goods entities must pay their annual membership fees and subscribe to the body's code."

> "Its industry code of conduct … is enforceable by law. Non-compliance with the code, accredited by the minister on 29 April 2015, is a breach of section 82 of the CPA."
