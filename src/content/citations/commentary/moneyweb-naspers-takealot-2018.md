# Moneyweb: Naspers bags all of Takealot

Short summary (not full reproduction): article (Hilton Tarrant, 25 June 2018) describes Naspers reaching effective 96% ownership of Takealot after acquiring Tiger Global's stake in a transaction completed in December 2017. Verbatim (re-checked against the live article 2026-11-17):

> "Naspers has acquired Tiger Global Management's stake in South Africa's largest e-commerce retailer Takealot, and now owns an effective 96% (91% fully diluted) of the business."

> "The December transaction followed the deal in August where Naspers invested $74 million (then worth approximately R1 billion) for a controlling stake in Takealot. Following this investment, it held an effective 58% interest in the business."

The article dates the December transaction to December 2017 ("The transaction, completed in December 2017"), so the controlling-stake deal it describes is August 2017. The site says "took a controlling stake in August 2017 and held an effective 96% from December 2017, as reported in June 2018"; it makes no claim about the percentage today. (Until 2026-11-18 the site said "Majority-owned … since December 2017", which dated the majority holding four months late.)

**The 96% is a 2018 figure.** It is the source used for the PoliciesSection copy, but it has not been checked against any later Naspers annual report, so it describes the position as reported in June 2018, not necessarily today. (Until 2026-11-17 this file called it "the most recent documented public figure", which was not checked.)
