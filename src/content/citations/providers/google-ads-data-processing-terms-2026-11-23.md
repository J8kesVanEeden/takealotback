# Google: where analytics data is processed, and the transfer terms

Google Analytics runs on this site through Cloudflare Zaraz (see the privacy note in About). These are Google's own published terms on where that data can go and what governs the transfer. None of them names South Africa.

Google handles analytics data in two roles. For the data it processes for the site, it is a processor under the Data Processing Terms below. Where it uses the data for its own purposes, it is a controller under separate controller terms (the Google Measurement Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms), whose transfer clauses ("4. Data Transfers") are written for data under European and UK law (read 2026-11-24). The privacy note's line on where data goes therefore rests on Google's privacy policy page on data transfers, read with section 10.1 of the processor terms below for the data Google processes on the site's behalf.

## Data transfer frameworks (effective 23 August 2025)

From https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks:

> "We maintain servers around the world and your information may be processed on servers located outside of the country where you live."

> "We rely on SCCs for our data transfers where required and in instances where they are not covered by an adequacy decision."

## Google Measurement Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms

From https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9012600 (retrieved 2026-11-23). These apply when the site owner turns on Google Analytics data sharing.

Definitions, "Data Sharing Setting":

> "the data sharing setting which Customer has enabled via the user interface of the Measurement Services and which enables Google and its Affiliates to use personal data for improving Google’s and its Affiliates’ products and services."

Section 4.1, Independent Controllers:

> "each: (a) is an independent controller of Controller Personal Data; (b) will individually determine the purposes and means of its processing of Controller Personal Data"

## Google Ads Data Processing Terms (Version 8.0, 30 May 2024)

These are Google's processor terms, for the data it handles on the site's behalf. Section 10.1, Data Storage and Processing Facilities:

> "Google may process Customer Personal Data in any country in which Google or its Subprocessors maintain facilities."

Appendix 2, section 3(a):

> "Google stores data in a multi-tenant environment on Google-owned servers."

## Advertising and analytics products: international data transfers (9 September 2024)

From https://business.safety.google/adsdatatransfers/. The page covers both sets of terms above, processor and controller:

> "In accordance with the Google Ads Data Processing Terms, Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms, and the Google Measurement Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms (as applicable), Google may rely on SCCs for certain data transfers in connection with our advertising and analytics products"

## Where the site uses this

The privacy note's line on data processed outside South Africa. "SCCs" are standard contractual clauses.
