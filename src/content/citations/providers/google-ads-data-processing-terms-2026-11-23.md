# Google: where analytics data is processed, and the transfer terms

Google Analytics runs on this site through Cloudflare Zaraz (see the privacy note in About). These are Google's own published terms on where that data can go and what governs the transfer. None of them names South Africa.

## Google Ads Data Processing Terms (Version 8.0, 30 May 2024)

Section 10.1, Data Storage and Processing Facilities:

> "Google may process Customer Personal Data in any country in which Google or its Subprocessors maintain facilities."

Appendix 2, section 3(a):

> "Google stores data in a multi-tenant environment on Google-owned servers."

## Data transfer frameworks (effective 23 August 2025)

From https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks:

> "your information may be processed on servers located outside of the country where you live"

> "We rely on SCCs for our data transfers where required and in instances where they are not covered by an adequacy decision."

## Advertising and analytics products: international data transfers (9 September 2024)

From https://business.safety.google/adsdatatransfers/:

> "Google may rely on SCCs for certain data transfers in connection with our advertising and analytics products"

## Where the site uses this

The privacy note's line on data processed outside South Africa. "SCCs" are standard contractual clauses.
