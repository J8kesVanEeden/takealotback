# Cloudflare: where data is stored, and the transfer terms

Cloudflare hosts this site and runs Cloudflare Web Analytics and Zaraz (see the privacy note in About). These are Cloudflare's own published terms on where data goes and what governs the transfer. Neither names South Africa.

## Privacy Policy (effective 4 November 2025), section 7: International information transfers

> "We primarily store your information in the United States and the European Economic Area."

> "we will implement appropriate safeguards, consistent with the laws of the territory from which the data is exported."

> "Cloudflare relies on the standard contractual clauses"

> "we rely on our certifications under the CBPR System and Global PRP System, as applicable"

## Data Processing Addendum (Version 6.4, effective 3 April 2026)

From https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/, clause 6.1:

> "Cloudflare (and its sub-Processors) may process outside of the European Economic Area"

Annex 2:

> "Cloudflare deploys high-availability systems across geographically-distributed data centers."

The privacy note's line on data processed outside South Africa.
